# Claim Notification and Submission Timelines

Current as of 1 April 2026. This document replaces the 2024 timelines sheet,
which is retained for reference only as `claims-timelines-2024-ARCHIVED` and
must not be quoted to customers.

## Notification

| Situation | Who notifies | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Planned admission, cashless | Hospital | 72 hours before admission |
| Emergency admission, cashless | Hospital | Within 24 hours of admission |
| Planned admission, reimbursement | Insured | 72 hours before admission |
| Emergency admission, reimbursement | Insured | Within 48 hours of admission |
| Death during hospitalisation | Insured's representative | Within 24 hours |

## Submission

| Document set | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Main reimbursement claim | 30 days from date of discharge |
| Pre-hospitalisation bills | With the main claim |
| Post-hospitalisation bills | 15 days after the end of the post-hospitalisation window |
| Response to an Aurora query | 45 days from the date of the query |

If the response to a query is not received within 45 days, Aurora closes the
claim. A closed claim can be reopened once, within 6 months, on written request.

## Aurora's response timelines

| Action | Aurora's deadline |
|---|---|
| Cashless pre-authorisation, emergency | 1 hour |
| Cashless pre-authorisation, planned | 4 working hours |
| Cashless final approval at discharge | 3 working hours |
| Reimbursement settlement or rejection | 15 working days from last document |
| Raising a query on a submitted claim | 7 working days from receipt |

## Delay condonation

A claim submitted after the 30-day window is not automatically rejected. Aurora
condones delay where the insured shows that submission within the window was
not reasonably possible — for example, continued hospitalisation of the insured,
a natural disaster, or a documented postal failure. Applications for condonation
must be made in writing and are decided by the claims committee within
21 working days.

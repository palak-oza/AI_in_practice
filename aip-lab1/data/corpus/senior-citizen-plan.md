# Aurora Senior Care — Plan for Ages 60 and Above

A separate retail product for entrants aged 60 to 80. **It is not one of the
four plans in `plans-overview`** and its terms are materially different.

## Sum insured

₹3,00,000, ₹5,00,000 or ₹10,00,000. Individual only; no family floater.

## Sub-limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Room rent | 1.5% of sum insured per day |
| ICU | 3% of sum insured per day |
| Cataract | ₹30,000 per eye |
| Knee or hip replacement | ₹1,75,000 per joint |
| Modern treatment | 40% of sum insured |
| Pre-hospitalisation | 30 days |
| Post-hospitalisation | 60 days |

## Co-payment

**Mandatory 20% co-payment on every claim**, at every age, with no option to
buy it out. This is the single largest difference from the retail plans and
the most common source of complaint.

## Waiting periods

| Category | Waiting period |
|---|---|
| Initial | 30 days |
| Specified illnesses | 24 months |
| Pre-existing disease | **24 months** (shorter than the 36 months on retail plans) |

## Medical underwriting

A pre-policy medical check-up is mandatory for all entrants. Aurora bears 100%
of the cost if the proposal is accepted, and 50% if it is declined.

## Renewability

Lifelong, with no exit age. Premium is revised in 5-year age bands.

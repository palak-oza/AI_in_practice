# Out-Patient Cover, Teleconsultation, and Wellness

## Out-patient benefit (OPD rider)

Aurora's core plans are in-patient indemnity products. Out-patient consultations,
diagnostics and pharmacy are **not** covered under the base plan on any tier.

The optional **OPD rider** covers them:

| Plan | OPD rider annual limit | Rider premium |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | Not available | — |
| Silver | ₹10,000 | 8% of base premium |
| Gold | ₹25,000 | 7% of base premium |
| Platinum | ₹50,000 | 6% of base premium |

The OPD rider covers doctor consultations at network clinics, prescribed
diagnostics, prescribed pharmacy, and one dental check-up and cleaning per year.
It does not cover spectacles, over-the-counter medicine, or wellness supplements.
There is no waiting period on the OPD rider; it is live from policy inception.

Reimbursement under the OPD rider requires the prescription and the bill to be
uploaded within 60 days of the consultation. Claims are settled within 7 working
days. Unused OPD limit does not carry forward.

## Teleconsultation

Unlimited teleconsultation with Aurora's empanelled general physicians is
included on **all plans including Bronze**, at no extra premium and with no
sub-limit. Specialist teleconsultation is included on Gold and Platinum;
on Silver it draws down the OPD rider limit if the rider is held, and is
otherwise chargeable.

A teleconsultation prescription is valid for a pharmacy claim under the OPD
rider on the same terms as an in-person prescription.

## Wellness programme

Wellness points are earned for verified activity, health assessments, and
preventive screening:

| Activity | Points per year (max) |
|---|---|
| Verified 10,000 steps/day, per qualifying day | 1 (max 250) |
| Annual health check-up completed | 150 |
| Preventive screening appropriate to age and sex | 100 |
| Quit-tobacco programme completed | 200 |
| Health risk assessment completed at renewal | 50 |

Points convert to a renewal-premium discount at 1,000 points = 5%, capped at
15%. Points do not carry forward between policy years, and are forfeited if the
policy lapses beyond the grace period.

# Aurora Gold — Plan Terms

Product UIN: AUR-HI-GLD-2026. Effective 1 April 2026.

## Sum insured

₹10,00,000 or ₹25,00,000, individual or family floater. Family floater covers
self, spouse, up to four dependent children under 25, and up to two dependent
parents.

## Sub-limits

Gold removes the room-rent and ICU sub-limits entirely. You may occupy any
single private room; only a suite or a deluxe room designated above the
hospital's single-private-room category is excluded. Because there is no
room-rent cap, **proportionate deduction never applies on Gold**.

| Item | Gold limit |
|---|---|
| Room rent | Single private room, no monetary cap |
| ICU charges | No cap |
| Cataract surgery | ₹75,000 per eye |
| Knee or hip replacement | No sub-limit |
| Modern treatment | Up to sum insured |
| Ambulance (road) | ₹5,000 per hospitalisation |
| Air ambulance | ₹2,50,000 per policy year, within India, on prior authorisation |

## Co-payment

Nil at all ages.

## Restoration

Unlimited restorations within a policy year. Unlike Silver, a restored sum
insured on Gold may be used for the same illness, provided the second
hospitalisation is at least 45 days after discharge from the first.

## No-claim bonus

25% of base sum insured per claim-free year, accumulating to a maximum of 100%.
The bonus is not reduced by a claim; it stops accumulating for that year only.

## Additional Gold benefits

- Annual preventive health check-up for every adult insured, every year,
  irrespective of claim history, up to ₹7,500 per adult.
- Domiciliary hospitalisation up to ₹1,00,000 where the treating doctor
  certifies in writing that hospital admission was not possible.
- Second medical opinion for 18 listed critical conditions, at no cost.

# ARCHIVED — Claim Timelines (2024 edition)

> **STATUS: SUPERSEDED on 1 April 2026.** Retained only for claims with a date
> of admission before 1 April 2026. Do not quote these figures for any current
> policy. The current document is `claims-timelines`.

## Notification (2024 rules)

| Situation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Planned admission, cashless | 48 hours before admission |
| Emergency admission, cashless | Within 24 hours of admission |
| Planned admission, reimbursement | 48 hours before admission |
| Emergency admission, reimbursement | Within 24 hours of admission |

## Submission (2024 rules)

| Document set | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Main reimbursement claim | 15 days from date of discharge |
| Post-hospitalisation bills | 7 days after the post-hospitalisation window |
| Response to an Aurora query | 30 days from the date of the query |

## Aurora response timelines (2024 rules)

| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Cashless pre-authorisation, planned | 6 working hours |
| Reimbursement settlement | 30 days from last document |

Delay condonation under the 2024 rules required approval from the regional
claims head rather than the claims committee.

# Network Hospitals and Cashless Access

Aurora's network covers 11,400 hospitals across 780 towns as of 1 April 2026.
The authoritative list is the hospital locator in the Aurora app and on the
portal; any printed or cached list is indicative only, because hospitals join
and leave the network continuously.

## Network tiers

| Tier | Description | Cashless available |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred Provider Network (PPN) | Negotiated package rates for 42 common procedures | Yes, at package rates |
| Standard network | Cashless at tariff | Yes |
| Excluded provider | Hospitals excluded for documented fraud or persistent over-billing | No — reimbursement only, and pre-approval required |

The excluded provider list is published quarterly on the portal. A claim from an
excluded provider is payable on reimbursement only if the admission was a
genuine emergency and no alternative facility was reachable.

## PPN package rates

Where a procedure is on the PPN list and the hospital is a PPN hospital, Aurora
pays the negotiated package rate and the hospital may not bill the insured for
the difference. Elective procedures on the PPN list performed at a PPN hospital
at above the package rate are settled at the package rate; the excess is not
payable and is not eligible for proportionate treatment.

## When cashless is denied

Cashless can be denied at pre-authorisation for four reasons, and denial of
cashless is **not** a rejection of the claim:

1. The information supplied is insufficient to establish medical necessity.
2. The condition appears to fall within an unexpired waiting period.
3. The policy is not in force on the date of admission.
4. The hospital is an excluded provider.

In cases 1 and 2 the insured may pay and file for reimbursement, where the same
facts are assessed against the full document set rather than a pre-authorisation
form. A material proportion of denied cashless requests are subsequently
settled on reimbursement.

## Minimum hospital definition

For a facility to qualify as a hospital under the policy it must have at least
10 in-patient beds in towns with a population below 10 lakh, or 15 in-patient
beds elsewhere; a qualified nursing staff present around the clock; a fully
equipped operation theatre where surgical procedures are performed; and daily
patient records accessible to Aurora's authorised personnel.

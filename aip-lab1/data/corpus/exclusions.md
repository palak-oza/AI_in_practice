# Permanent Exclusions

The following are never payable under any Aurora indemnity plan, at any sum
insured, regardless of waiting periods served.

## Treatment-related exclusions

- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless required to treat an accidental injury,
  burn, or cancer, and certified as medically necessary by the treating surgeon.
- Any treatment for obesity or weight control, other than bariatric surgery that
  meets the criteria in `pre-existing-conditions`.
- Infertility, assisted reproduction, IVF, surrogacy, and sterilisation reversal.
- Gender-affirming surgery, unless mandated by a court or by law.
- Dental treatment and oral surgery, unless necessitated by an accidental injury
  requiring hospitalisation.
- Refractive error correction where the error is less than 7.5 dioptres.
- Hearing aids, spectacles, contact lenses, and their fitting.
- Experimental, investigational, or unproven treatment, defined as any treatment
  not supported by prevailing Indian medical practice.
- Rest cure, respite care, custodial care, and convalescence at any facility.
- Treatment taken outside India, except under Platinum's international
  emergency benefit.

## Conduct-related exclusions

- Injury or illness arising from participation in a criminal act.
- Self-inflicted injury and attempted suicide, whether sane or insane.
- Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, and any illness or injury
  directly attributable to intoxication.
- Injury sustained while participating in hazardous activities: professional
  sport, motor racing, scuba diving below 30 metres, mountaineering requiring
  ropes, parachuting, and hang-gliding.
- War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, and nuclear contamination.

## Expense-related exclusions (non-payable items)

Registration and admission charges; telephone, television, internet and laundry;
attendant and visitor charges; toiletries and cosmetics; documentation and
record charges; ambulance beyond the ₹5,000 limit; and the cost of a companion's
food or bed except where the patient is a minor under 12.

## Two things people expect to be excluded but are not

- **Mental illness.** In-patient treatment for mental illness is covered on the
  same terms as physical illness, as required by the Mental Healthcare Act 2017.
  The 30-day initial waiting period applies; no separate waiting period does.
- **HIV and AIDS.** Covered from policy inception, subject only to the standard
  pre-existing disease rules if diagnosed before the policy start.

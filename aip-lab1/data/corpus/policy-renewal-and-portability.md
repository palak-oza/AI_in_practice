# Renewal, Grace Period, and Portability

## Renewal

All Aurora indemnity plans are lifelong renewable. Aurora may not decline
renewal on grounds of the insured's age, claims history, or adverse change in
health. Renewal may be declined only for established fraud, misrepresentation,
or non-disclosure of a material fact.

Premium is not guaranteed. Aurora may revise premium rates with the approval of
the regulator, but only for an entire product cohort, never for an individual
policyholder on the basis of their claims.

## Grace period

The grace period is **30 days** from the renewal due date for annual policies
and **15 days** for policies paid in instalments.

Cover is **not** in force during the grace period. A claim for an admission that
occurs inside the grace period, before the renewal premium is received, is not
payable. What the grace period preserves is *continuity*: renew inside it and
your waiting periods and no-claim bonus carry forward unbroken.

Renew after the grace period expires and the policy is treated as fresh. All
waiting periods restart, accumulated no-claim bonus is lost, and fresh
underwriting applies.

## Portability

You may port an existing health policy from another Indian insurer to Aurora at
your renewal date, keeping the waiting periods you have already served.

- Apply at least **45 days** and no more than **60 days** before your existing
  policy's renewal date.
- Aurora must accept or decline within **15 days** of receiving the complete
  portability application and the previous insurer's data.
- If Aurora does not respond within 15 days, the application is deemed accepted.
- Credit for waiting periods served is granted **only up to the sum insured of
  the previous policy**, including its accrued no-claim bonus. Any increase in
  sum insured is subject to fresh waiting periods on the incremental amount.
- Portability is not available mid-term, only at renewal.

## Migration

Migration is moving between Aurora's own products — for example Silver to Gold.
The rules are the same as portability with respect to waiting-period credit, but
no 45-day notice is required; migration can be requested at any renewal.

## Free-look period

A new policyholder may cancel within **15 days** of receiving the policy
document (**30 days** if the policy was sold through distance marketing) and
receive a refund of premium less proportionate risk premium for the period of
cover, medical examination costs, and stamp duty.

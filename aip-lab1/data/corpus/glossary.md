# Glossary

**AYUSH** — Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy.

**Cashless facility** — Settlement made by Aurora directly to a network hospital,
so that the insured does not pay and then claim.

**Co-payment** — A fixed percentage of every admissible claim that the insured
bears. Distinct from a deductible, which is an absolute amount.

**Cumulative bonus / No-claim bonus** — An increase in sum insured granted for a
claim-free year, at no additional premium.

**Day-care procedure** — A procedure that requires less than 24 hours of
hospitalisation because of technological advance, and is therefore payable
despite the general 24-hour admission requirement. Out-patient treatment is not
a day-care procedure.

**Deductible** — An absolute amount the insured bears before cover begins. Aurora's
indemnity plans do not carry a deductible; its top-up products do.

**Domiciliary hospitalisation** — Treatment at home for a condition that would
normally require hospitalisation, where the patient could not be moved or a bed
was unavailable.

**Free-look period** — The window in which a new policy can be returned for a
refund.

**Grace period** — The window after the due date in which a renewal premium may
be paid without losing continuity. Cover does not operate during it.

**Indemnity** — Reimbursement of actual expenses incurred, up to a limit, as
opposed to a fixed payout on the occurrence of an event.

**Migration** — Moving between products of the same insurer with waiting-period
credit.

**Network hospital** — A hospital with which Aurora has an agreement to provide
cashless treatment.

**Portability** — Moving to a different insurer with waiting-period credit.

**Pre-existing disease (PED)** — A condition diagnosed, treated, or symptomatic
and known to the insured in the 48 months before policy commencement.

**Pre- and post-hospitalisation** — Related medical expenses incurred in a
defined window before admission and after discharge, payable as part of the same
claim.

**Proportionate deduction** — Reduction of associated hospital charges in the
ratio of the eligible room rent to the actual room rent.

**Restoration** — Automatic reinstatement of an exhausted sum insured within the
same policy year.

**Sub-limit** — A cap on a specific head of expense that sits below the overall
sum insured.

**Sum insured (SI)** — The maximum Aurora will pay in a policy year, before any
no-claim bonus or restoration.

**Third Party Administrator (TPA)** — An intermediary that processes claims on
an insurer's behalf. Aurora administers its own claims and does not use a TPA.

**UIN** — Unique Identification Number allotted to an insurance product by the
regulator.

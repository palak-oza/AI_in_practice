# Dependents and the Family Floater

## Who can be covered

| Relationship | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self and spouse | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Dependent children under 25 | Up to 2 | Up to 3 | Up to 4 | Up to 4 |
| Dependent parents | No | Yes, at extra premium | Yes | Yes |
| Dependent parents-in-law | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Siblings, grandparents | No | No | No | No |

A child ceases to be a dependent at 25, or earlier on marriage or on becoming
financially independent. Aurora offers guaranteed continuation into an
individual policy at that point, with all waiting periods carried over, provided
the request is made within 90 days.

## How the floater sum insured works

A family floater is a single shared pool. If a ₹10,00,000 Silver floater covers
four people and one member claims ₹8,00,000, only ₹2,00,000 remains for the
other three for the rest of the policy year — until restoration triggers.

This is the trade-off people misunderstand most often. A floater is cheaper than
four individual policies of the same sum insured because the probability of two
large simultaneous claims is low, not because it provides the same cover.

## Adding and removing members

- **Marriage**: a spouse may be added mid-term, within 60 days of the marriage,
  on payment of pro-rata premium. Fresh waiting periods apply to the spouse.
- **Birth or adoption**: see `maternity-benefits` for the 90-day window.
- **Death of a member**: the policy continues for the remaining members for the
  rest of the policy year at no change in premium. If the deceased was the
  proposer, the eldest remaining adult becomes the proposer on request.
- **Divorce**: the former spouse must be moved to a separate policy at the next
  renewal; waiting periods served carry over.

## Age bands and loading

Premium is determined by the age of the **eldest** member covered. Adding a
dependent parent to a floater therefore often costs more than an individual
senior-citizen policy for that parent, and Aurora's advisers are required to
present both options in writing before the sale.

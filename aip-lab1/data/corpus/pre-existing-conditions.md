# Waiting Periods and Pre-Existing Diseases

A waiting period is a stretch of continuous coverage that must elapse before a
particular kind of claim becomes payable. Waiting periods run from the date of
first policy commencement, not from each renewal, provided coverage has been
continuous without a break of more than 30 days.

## Schedule of waiting periods

| Category | Waiting period | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Initial waiting period | 30 days | Any illness other than accident |
| Accidental injury | Nil | From day one |
| Specified illnesses and procedures | 24 months | See list below |
| Pre-existing diseases (PED) | 36 months | Any condition diagnosed or treated in the 48 months before the policy start |
| Maternity | 24 months | See `maternity-benefits` |
| Bariatric surgery | 36 months | Where medically indicated and BMI criteria met |

## Specified illnesses with a 24-month waiting period

Cataract; benign prostatic hypertrophy; hernia of all types; hydrocele; fissure,
fistula or haemorrhoids; gall bladder and bile duct stones; kidney and urinary
tract stones; benign uterine conditions including fibroids; varicose veins;
tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy; sinusitis and deviated nasal septum surgery;
knee and hip joint replacement other than following an accident; and all
internal congenital anomalies.

## What counts as pre-existing

A condition is pre-existing if, in the 48 months before the policy start date,
it was diagnosed by a registered practitioner, or the insured received medical
advice or treatment for it, or its signs or symptoms were present and known to
the insured.

Hypertension, diabetes mellitus, and cardiac conditions declared at proposal
stage are treated as pre-existing and carry the 36-month waiting period unless
Aurora explicitly waives it in writing on the policy schedule.

## Reduction of the PED waiting period

Aurora offers a **PED Waiting Period Reduction** rider that reduces 36 months to
either 24 months or 12 months, at additional premium. The rider must be bought
at inception or at the first renewal; it cannot be added later.

## Portability

Waiting periods already served with a previous Indian insurer carry over on
portability, up to the sum insured of the previous policy. The excess sum
insured is subject to fresh waiting periods. See `policy-renewal-and-portability`.

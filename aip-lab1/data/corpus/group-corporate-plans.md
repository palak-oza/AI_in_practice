# Aurora Group Health — Corporate Plan Comparison

For employer-sponsored group policies with 50 or more lives. **Individual and
family-floater retail plans are covered in `plans-overview` and the terms
differ substantially.**

## Plan variants

| Feature | Group Essential | Group Advantage | Group Premier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sum insured per employee | ₹2L–₹5L | ₹5L–₹15L | ₹15L–₹50L |
| Room rent | 1.5% of SI | Single private room | No limit |
| Pre-existing diseases | Covered from day one | Covered from day one | Covered from day one |
| Waiting periods | All waived | All waived | All waived |
| Maternity | ₹50,000 | ₹1,00,000 | ₹2,00,000, no waiting period |
| Co-payment | 10% | Nil | Nil |
| Parents covered | Optional, at extra premium | Optional | Included |
| Corporate buffer | No | ₹10L floating | ₹50L floating |

The waiver of all waiting periods, including for pre-existing disease, is the
defining feature of group cover and the main reason employees are advised not
to let a group policy lapse when changing jobs.

## Enrolment

New joiners are added from the date of joining, with no medical underwriting,
if enrolled within 30 days. After 30 days the employee waits until the annual
enrolment window.

## On leaving employment

Cover ceases on the last working day. Aurora offers conversion to a retail
policy with waiting-period credit for the period covered under the group plan,
provided the application is made within 45 days of exit.

## Claims

Group claims follow the same cashless and reimbursement routes as retail, but
the reimbursement submission window is 60 days from discharge rather than 30,
and the employer's HR desk is the first point of contact.

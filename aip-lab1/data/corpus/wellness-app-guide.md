# Aurora App — User Guide

How to use the app. Policy terms are not in this document.

## Getting started

Download from the Play Store or App Store, register with the mobile number on
your policy, and verify by OTP. If the OTP does not arrive, check that the
number on the policy matches; update it under Profile > Contact Details or
through customer service.

## What you can do in the app

- View and download your e-card and policy schedule
- Download the 80D premium certificate
- Find network hospitals with the locator
- Start a cashless pre-authorisation
- Upload reimbursement documents and track claim status
- Book a teleconsultation
- Sync a fitness tracker for wellness points
- Raise and track a grievance

## Uploading claim documents

Photograph each document flat, in good light, in full. The uploader accepts
JPG, PNG and PDF up to 10 MB per file and 25 files per claim. Blurred or
partial images are the most common cause of a claim query, and a query
restarts the settlement clock.

## Wellness tracker sync

Supported: Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin. Steps sync once daily at
midnight IST. Manually entered steps do not earn points.

## Known issues

- App version 4.x on Android 14 can crash when uploading files over 8 MB.
  Compress the file or upload from the web portal.
- The hospital locator returns no results if location permission is denied;
  search by city name instead.
- E-card download fails on iOS if the device storage is below 200 MB.

## Support

In-app chat 24×7, or the helpline number on your policy schedule.

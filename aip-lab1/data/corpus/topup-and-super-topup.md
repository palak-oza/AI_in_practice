# Aurora Top-Up and Super Top-Up Plans

Top-up products sit above a deductible and are bought alongside a base health
policy. They are **not** the same as the indemnity plans in `plans-overview`,
and the sub-limits and waiting periods below apply only to the top-up.

## The difference between top-up and super top-up

- **Top-up**: the deductible applies to **each single claim**. A ₹5L deductible
  with two ₹4L claims pays nothing.
- **Super top-up**: the deductible applies to the **aggregate of all claims in
  the policy year**. The same two ₹4L claims exceed ₹5L in aggregate, so ₹3L is
  payable.

Super top-up is almost always the correct choice and costs about 20% more.

## Deductible and sum insured options

| Deductible | Sum insured options |
|---|---|
| ₹3,00,000 | ₹5L, ₹10L, ₹20L |
| ₹5,00,000 | ₹10L, ₹20L, ₹50L |
| ₹10,00,000 | ₹20L, ₹50L, ₹1Cr |

## Waiting periods

| Category | Waiting period |
|---|---|
| Initial | 30 days |
| Specified illnesses | 24 months |
| Pre-existing disease | 36 months |
| Maternity | Not covered on any top-up variant |

Waiting periods on the top-up run independently of the base policy. Serving
36 months on a base policy does **not** waive the top-up's PED waiting period.
This is the most common misunderstanding at claim stage.

## Room rent

No room-rent sub-limit on any top-up variant. Proportionate deduction does not
apply.

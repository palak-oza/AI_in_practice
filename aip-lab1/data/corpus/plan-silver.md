# Aurora Silver — Plan Terms

Product UIN: AUR-HI-SIL-2026. Effective 1 April 2026.

## Sum insured and premium band

Silver is available at sum insured of ₹5,00,000 or ₹10,00,000, on an individual
or family-floater basis. Family floater covers self, spouse, and up to three
dependent children under 25.

## Sub-limits

| Item | Silver limit |
|---|---|
| Room rent | 2% of sum insured per day |
| ICU charges | 4% of sum insured per day |
| Cataract surgery | ₹40,000 per eye |
| Knee or hip replacement | ₹2,50,000 per joint |
| Modern treatment (robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, stem cell therapy) | 50% of sum insured |
| Ambulance | ₹5,000 per hospitalisation |

**Proportionate deduction.** If you occupy a room whose rent exceeds the 2%
limit, Aurora reduces *all* associated charges — surgeon fees, anaesthetist,
nursing, operation theatre — in the same proportion, not just the room charge.
This is the most frequent cause of complaint and is worked through with a
numerical example in `claims-process`.

## Co-payment

A 10% co-payment applies to every claim where the eldest insured member is
above 60 years at the time of the policy commencement or renewal. There is no
co-payment below age 60.

## Restoration

The sum insured is restored once per policy year after it is fully exhausted.
The restored amount may not be used for the same illness for the same insured
person in the same policy year.

## No-claim bonus

10% of the base sum insured is added for each claim-free year, up to a maximum
accumulation of 50%. A claim in any year reduces the accumulated bonus by 10
percentage points; it does not reset it to zero.

## Waiting periods

Standard waiting periods apply. See `pre-existing-conditions` for the complete
schedule; Silver does not shorten or extend any of them.

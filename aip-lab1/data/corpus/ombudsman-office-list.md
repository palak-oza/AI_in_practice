this document only lists jurisdictions.

Complaints are filed with the office having jurisdiction over the branch or
office of the insurer complained against, or over the complainant's place of
residence.

The Council for Insurance Ombudsmen publishes the current addresses; this list
is territorial jurisdiction only and is updated annually.

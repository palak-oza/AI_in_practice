# Filing a Claim

There are two routes. Which one is available depends on whether the hospital is
in the Aurora network, not on the plan you hold.

## Route 1 — Cashless (network hospitals only)

1. Present your Aurora health card and a government photo ID at the hospital
   insurance desk.
2. The hospital submits a pre-authorisation request to Aurora.
3. For a **planned** admission, submit the request at least **72 hours** before
   admission. Aurora responds within **4 working hours** of receiving a complete
   request.
4. For an **emergency** admission, the hospital must notify Aurora within
   **24 hours** of admission. Aurora responds within **1 hour** for emergencies.
5. Aurora settles the approved amount directly with the hospital. You pay only
   the non-payable items, any co-payment, and anything above the sum insured.

A pre-authorisation approval is an estimate, not a final settlement. The final
amount is decided on discharge against the actual bill.

## Route 2 — Reimbursement (any hospital)

Use this at non-network hospitals, or where cashless was denied.

1. Notify Aurora within **48 hours** of an emergency admission, or **72 hours
   before** a planned admission. Notification is by app, portal, or the 24×7
   helpline. Failure to notify does not by itself void the claim but must be
   explained.
2. Pay the hospital yourself and collect the complete document set.
3. Submit the claim within **30 days of discharge**.
4. Submit post-hospitalisation bills within **15 days** of the end of the
   post-hospitalisation period applicable to your plan.

## Documents required for reimbursement

- Completed and signed claim form (Part A by you, Part B by the hospital)
- Original discharge summary
- Original itemised hospital bill and payment receipt
- All investigation reports and their prescriptions
- Pharmacy bills with the corresponding prescriptions
- Implant sticker and invoice, where an implant was used
- First Information Report, in case of accident or injury
- Cancelled cheque or bank passbook first page for NEFT
- KYC documents where the claim exceeds ₹1,00,000

## Settlement timelines

Aurora settles or rejects a complete reimbursement claim within **15 working
days** of receiving the last required document. If Aurora raises a query, the
clock restarts when you supply the queried document. Where Aurora exceeds the
regulatory settlement timeline, interest is payable at the bank rate plus 2%.

## Worked example — proportionate deduction

An insured on Silver with a ₹5,00,000 sum insured has a room-rent limit of
2%, i.e. ₹10,000 per day. They occupy a room at ₹15,000 per day for 4 days.
The eligibility ratio is 10,000 ÷ 15,000 = 0.667.

| Head | Billed | Payable |
|---|---|---|
| Room rent (4 days) | ₹60,000 | ₹40,000 |
| Surgeon and anaesthetist | ₹1,20,000 | ₹80,000 |
| Nursing and OT | ₹45,000 | ₹30,000 |
| Investigations | ₹30,000 | ₹20,000 |
| Pharmacy and consumables | ₹25,000 | ₹25,000 |
| **Total** | **₹2,80,000** | **₹1,95,000** |

Pharmacy and consumables are not proportionately reduced. Everything tied to
the room category is. The insured pays ₹85,000 out of pocket on a claim that
was well within the sum insured — which is the point of the example.

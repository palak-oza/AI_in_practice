# Aurora Bronze — Plan Terms

Product UIN: AUR-HI-BRZ-2026. Effective 1 April 2026.

Bronze is Aurora's entry-level indemnity plan. It is designed for buyers whose
priority is the lowest possible premium and who accept meaningful sub-limits.

## Sum insured

₹3,00,000 or ₹5,00,000. Individual or family floater covering self, spouse and
up to two dependent children under 25. Dependent parents cannot be added to a
Bronze floater; they require a separate policy.

## Sub-limits

| Item | Bronze limit |
|---|---|
| Room rent | 1% of sum insured per day |
| ICU charges | 2% of sum insured per day |
| Cataract surgery | ₹25,000 per eye |
| Knee or hip replacement | ₹1,50,000 per joint |
| Modern treatment | 25% of sum insured |
| Ambulance | ₹5,000 per hospitalisation |
| Maternity | Not covered |

On a ₹3,00,000 sum insured the room-rent limit is ₹3,000 per day, which is
below the single-private-room tariff at most metropolitan tertiary hospitals.
Proportionate deduction will therefore apply to most Bronze claims in those
hospitals. Buyers should read the worked example in `claims-process` before
choosing Bronze.

## Co-payment

20% co-payment on every claim where the eldest insured member is above 60 at
commencement or renewal.

## Restoration

Not available on Bronze.

## No-claim bonus

5% per claim-free year, maximum accumulation 25%.

## Day-care procedures

150 listed day-care procedures are covered. Procedures outside the list require
a minimum 24-hour admission to qualify.

# Regulatory Disclosures

Statutory information. Not a description of policy benefits.

## Product filing

All Aurora retail health products are filed with the regulator under the Use
and File procedure. Each product carries a Unique Identification Number (UIN)
printed on the policy schedule and in the plan document.

## Solvency

Aurora maintains a solvency ratio above the regulatory minimum of 1.50. The
ratio as at the last audited quarter is published in the Public Disclosures
section of the corporate website, updated quarterly.

## Grievance statistics

Complaint volumes, disposal rates, and average turnaround time are published
quarterly. The Ombudsman's annual report separately publishes awards against
each insurer.

## Free-look and cancellation rights

Statutory rights under the applicable regulations, reproduced in
`policy-renewal-and-portability`.

## Nomination

Nomination under Section 39 of the Insurance Act, 1938 is available on all
policies. A nomination made before the commencement of a policy year stands
until changed in writing.

## Anti-money-laundering and KYC

KYC documents are required at proposal stage for premiums above the prescribed
threshold, and for all claims above ₹1,00,000. Aurora is required to report
suspicious transactions.

## Data protection

Personal data is processed under Aurora's published privacy notice. Policyholders
may request access to, or correction of, their data through the grievance channel.

## Fair practices

Aurora's advisers are required to present suitable alternatives in writing where
a materially different product would better serve the customer, and to record
the customer's acknowledgement.

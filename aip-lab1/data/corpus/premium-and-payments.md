# Premium, Payment, and Tax

## Payment modes

Annual, half-yearly, quarterly, and monthly. Instalment options carry a loading:
half-yearly 2%, quarterly 3%, monthly 5% over the annual premium. Instalment
policies have a 15-day grace period rather than 30.

Payment by credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, NACH mandate, and cheque.
Cash is accepted only at branch and only up to ₹50,000 per transaction.

## Discounts

| Discount | Amount | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Family discount | 10% | Two or more members on one policy |
| Long-term policy | 7.5% for 2 years, 10% for 3 years | Paid upfront |
| Digital purchase | 5% | Bought on the app or portal without an intermediary |
| Wellness discount | Up to 15% at renewal | Based on wellness points, see `outpatient-and-wellness` |
| Loyalty | 5% | Third and subsequent consecutive renewals |

Total discounts are capped at 30% of base premium. The wellness discount is
applied last, after the cap, and is the only discount that may exceed it.

## Tax treatment

Premium paid for a health insurance policy qualifies for deduction under
Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961, in the old tax regime. The deduction is
₹25,000 for self, spouse and children where the eldest insured is below 60, and
₹50,000 where the eldest insured is 60 or above. An additional deduction of the
same amounts is available for a separate policy covering parents.

Preventive health check-up expenses of up to ₹5,000 are included within these
limits, and may be paid in cash — unlike premium, which must be paid by a
non-cash mode to qualify.

Aurora issues an 80D certificate in the app within 48 hours of payment. Aurora
does not provide tax advice; the treatment above is a general summary and the
new tax regime does not permit the 80D deduction at all.

## Refunds on cancellation

Cancellation by the insured after the free-look period attracts a short-period
scale: 75% refunded if cancelled within 1 month, 50% within 3 months, 25% within
6 months, and nil thereafter. No refund is payable if a claim has been made in
the policy year.

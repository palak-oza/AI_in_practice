# Aurora Motor Insurance — Claim Timelines

Applies to Aurora Motor (private car and two-wheeler) policies only. **This is
a different product line from Aurora Health.** Health policy timelines are in
`claims-timelines` and the numbers are not the same.

## Notification

| Situation | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Own-damage claim | Within 24 hours of the incident |
| Third-party claim | Within 24 hours, and an FIR within 48 hours |
| Theft | Immediately, and an FIR the same day |
| Windscreen only | Within 7 days |

## Submission

| Document set | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Main claim with surveyor report | 21 days from the date of loss |
| Repair estimate | With the claim |
| Response to an Aurora query | 30 days from the date of the query |

## Aurora's response timelines

| Action | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Surveyor appointment | 24 hours from intimation |
| Surveyor report | 15 days from appointment |
| Settlement or rejection | 30 days from the surveyor report |

## Cashless repair

Available at Aurora's 4,200 network garages. The garage raises the estimate,
Aurora approves within 4 working hours, and settles directly less the
compulsory deductible and depreciation on parts.

# Aurora Health Insurance — Plan Comparison

Effective 1 April 2026. Supersedes all comparison sheets dated before this.

Aurora offers four individual and family-floater health indemnity plans. All four
are indemnity products; none is a fixed-benefit or critical-illness policy.

## At a glance

| Feature | Bronze | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sum insured options | ₹3L, ₹5L | ₹5L, ₹10L | ₹10L, ₹25L | ₹25L, ₹50L, ₹1Cr |
| Room rent limit | 1% of SI per day | 2% of SI per day | Single private room | No limit (suite excluded) |
| ICU limit | 2% of SI per day | 4% of SI per day | No limit | No limit |
| Co-payment | 20% above age 60 | 10% above age 60 | Nil | Nil |
| Pre-hospitalisation cover | 30 days | 45 days | 60 days | 90 days |
| Post-hospitalisation cover | 60 days | 90 days | 120 days | 180 days |
| Restoration of sum insured | Not available | Once per year | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Day-care procedures | 150 listed | 400 listed | All | All |
| Annual health check-up | After 3 claim-free years | After 2 claim-free years | Every year | Every year |
| No-claim bonus | 5% per year, max 25% | 10% per year, max 50% | 25% per year, max 100% | 50% per year, max 200% |

## What is common to all plans

- Ambulance cover of ₹5,000 per hospitalisation, road ambulance only.
- AYUSH treatment (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Homeopathy) covered up to the
  sum insured when taken as in-patient at a government or NABH-accredited facility.
- Organ donor expenses covered up to the sum insured for the harvesting procedure.
- Lifelong renewability, subject to the terms in `policy-renewal-and-portability`.

## What is not covered by any plan

See `exclusions` for the complete list. The most commonly misunderstood items
are cosmetic surgery, infertility treatment, and out-patient dental work.

## Choosing between plans

Bronze exists to satisfy a minimum-cover requirement at the lowest premium; its
room-rent sub-limit means a private room in a metro tertiary hospital will
usually trigger proportionate deduction. Silver is the recommended entry plan
for a family. Gold removes room-rent and ICU sub-limits, which is the single
largest source of unexpected out-of-pocket cost. Platinum adds international
emergency cover, described in `plan-platinum-addendum`.

# Aurora Travel Insurance — Exclusions

Applies to Aurora Travel single-trip and multi-trip policies. **Not applicable
to Aurora Health indemnity plans**, whose exclusions are in `exclusions`.

## Permanent exclusions

- Pre-existing medical conditions, except life-threatening emergencies up to
  ₹1,00,000 on the Platinum travel variant.
- Treatment in the country of residence.
- Cosmetic and elective treatment of any kind.
- Pregnancy and childbirth, including complications, after the 26th week.
- Injuries from adventure sports unless the Adventure add-on was purchased:
  skiing, bungee jumping, white-water rafting, paragliding.
- Loss of baggage not reported to the carrier within 4 hours.
- Trip cancellation for a reason known at the time of booking.
- Travel against the advice of a registered medical practitioner.
- Travel to a country under a government travel advisory at the time of departure.

## Sub-limits

| Head | Silver Travel | Gold Travel |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency medical | USD 100,000 | USD 500,000 |
| Emergency dental | USD 500 | USD 1,000 |
| Baggage loss | USD 500 | USD 1,500 |
| Trip delay | USD 100 after 6 hours | USD 300 after 4 hours |
| Passport loss | USD 250 | USD 500 |

## Claim window

Notify within 48 hours of the incident. Submit within 30 days of return to
India, with original bills, boarding passes, and the passport page showing
entry and exit stamps.

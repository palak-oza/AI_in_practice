# Aurora Critical Illness Rider

A fixed-benefit rider payable as a lump sum on first diagnosis of a listed
condition. **It does not reimburse hospital bills**; the indemnity plans in
`plans-overview` do that, and both can be held together.

## Covered conditions (22 listed)

Cancer of specified severity; myocardial infarction; open chest CABG; stroke
resulting in permanent symptoms; kidney failure requiring regular dialysis;
major organ or bone marrow transplant; multiple sclerosis with persisting
symptoms; permanent paralysis of limbs; motor neurone disease; benign brain
tumour; blindness; deafness; loss of speech; third-degree burns; coma of
specified severity; aplastic anaemia; end-stage lung disease; end-stage liver
failure; Parkinson's disease before age 55; Alzheimer's disease before age 55;
primary pulmonary hypertension; and major head trauma.

## Sum insured

₹5,00,000 to ₹50,00,000, not exceeding the base policy sum insured.

## Waiting period and survival period

- **Initial waiting period: 90 days** from rider commencement. A diagnosis
  within 90 days is not payable and the rider is cancelled with a premium refund.
- **Survival period: 30 days** from the date of diagnosis. The insured must
  survive 30 days for the benefit to become payable.
- Pre-existing conditions: 48 months.

## After a claim

The rider terminates on payment of the full sum insured. The base indemnity
policy continues unaffected, including its no-claim bonus, because a critical
illness payout is not an indemnity claim.

## Premium

Level premium for 5-year bands, revised at each band boundary. Not guaranteed.

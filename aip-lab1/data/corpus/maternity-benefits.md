# Maternity and Newborn Benefits

Maternity cover is available on Silver, Gold and Platinum. It is **not**
available on Bronze at any sum insured.

## Waiting period

24 months of continuous coverage from the first policy commencement date. The
waiting period is served by the policy, not by the individual, so a spouse added
at the first renewal must still wait until 24 months from the original policy
start have elapsed.

## Limits per delivery

| Plan | Normal delivery | Caesarean section |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | ₹40,000 | ₹60,000 |
| Gold | ₹75,000 | ₹1,00,000 |
| Platinum | ₹1,50,000 | ₹2,00,000 |

The limit is per delivery and applies to a maximum of two deliveries in the
lifetime of the policy, whether or not they occur in consecutive policy years.

## What is included in the maternity limit

Pre-natal consultations and investigations in the 30 days before delivery;
delivery charges including the obstetrician and anaesthetist; hospital room and
nursing for the mother; and post-natal care for 60 days after delivery. All of
these draw down the same per-delivery limit; they are not separate benefits.

## Newborn cover

A newborn is covered from day one of birth, within the maternity limit, for the
first 90 days. Cover in this period includes treatment for congenital
conditions detected after birth, up to ₹50,000 on Silver and up to the maternity
limit on Gold and Platinum.

To continue cover beyond 90 days, the newborn must be added to the policy as a
dependent. Aurora must be informed within 90 days of birth. Adding the newborn
is guaranteed-issue — no medical underwriting — if requested within that window;
after it, the child is treated as a fresh applicant.

## Termination of pregnancy

Voluntary termination within the first 12 weeks is excluded. Medically indicated
termination, and complications of pregnancy such as ectopic pregnancy,
pre-eclampsia and miscarriage, are covered under the main hospitalisation
benefit and do **not** draw down the maternity limit.

# Aurora Hospital Cash Benefit

A fixed daily allowance during hospitalisation, payable **in addition to** any
indemnity claim on the same admission. It exists to cover the things the
indemnity policy excludes — attendant costs, travel, lost income.

## Daily benefit options

| Option | Per day | ICU per day | Maximum days per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | ₹1,000 | ₹2,000 | 30 |
| B | ₹2,000 | ₹4,000 | 45 |
| C | ₹5,000 | ₹10,000 | 60 |

## Conditions

- A minimum continuous hospitalisation of **48 hours** is required. A 30-hour
  admission pays nothing, even where the indemnity claim is payable.
- The first day of each admission is a deductible day and is not paid.
- Maximum 15 consecutive days for any single admission on Options A and B.
- Day-care procedures do not qualify, regardless of the indemnity treatment.

## Waiting periods

30 days initial; 24 months for specified illnesses; 36 months for pre-existing
disease. Accident from day one.

## Claim

Submit the discharge summary and the indemnity claim reference. No bills are
required, because the benefit is not linked to expenditure. Settlement within
7 working days of the indemnity claim being settled.

# Aurora Life — Grace Period, Lapse and Revival

Applies to Aurora Life term and endowment policies. **Health insurance grace
periods are different and are described in `policy-renewal-and-portability`.**

## Grace period

| Premium mode | Grace period |
|---|---|
| Annual, half-yearly, quarterly | 30 days |
| Monthly | 15 days |

Unlike health insurance, **life cover continues during the grace period**. If
the life assured dies within the grace period, the claim is paid less the
outstanding premium.

## Lapse

A policy lapses if premium is unpaid at the end of the grace period. A lapsed
term policy provides no cover. A lapsed endowment policy that has acquired a
surrender value continues as a paid-up policy at a reduced sum assured.

## Revival

A lapsed policy can be revived within **5 years** of the first unpaid premium,
on payment of arrears with interest at 9.5% per annum compounded half-yearly,
and satisfactory evidence of continued insurability.

Revival within 6 months requires no fresh medical evidence. Beyond 6 months,
Aurora may require a medical examination at the policyholder's cost.

## Free-look

15 days from receipt, or 30 days for policies sold through distance marketing.

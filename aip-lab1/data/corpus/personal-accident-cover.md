# Aurora Personal Accident Cover

A fixed-benefit product. It pays a defined sum on a defined event, and does not
reimburse expenses. **Aurora's health plans are indemnity products and work
entirely differently** — see `glossary` for the distinction.

## Benefits

| Event | Payout |
|---|---|
| Accidental death | 100% of sum insured |
| Permanent total disablement | 100% of sum insured |
| Loss of both limbs or both eyes | 100% |
| Loss of one limb or one eye | 50% |
| Permanent partial disablement | Per the scale in the policy schedule |
| Temporary total disablement | 1% of SI per week, max 100 weeks |
| Education benefit for dependent children | ₹1,00,000 per child, max 2 |
| Funeral expenses | ₹50,000 |

## Sum insured

₹5L to ₹1Cr, subject to a maximum of 10 times annual income.

## Claim intimation

Within 30 days of the accident for death and disablement claims. Within 7 days
for temporary total disablement. Documents: FIR, post-mortem report where
applicable, disability certificate from a civil surgeon, and income proof.

## Exclusions

Suicide and self-inflicted injury; intoxication; participation in a criminal
act; war; nuclear risk; aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a
licensed carrier; and pre-existing disablement.

There is no waiting period on a personal accident policy. Cover starts from
the policy commencement date.
